About Me

Merging the exactness of biological science with low-level systems programming and high-performance software engineering.

Bio & Background

I am a Bioinformatics Master's student at Howest University of Applied Sciences (Belgium), holding a First Class Honours B.Sc. in Biological Sciences from Università degli Studi di Napoli "Parthenope" (Naples, Italy).

Throughout my academic and professional journey—spanning life sciences research, ultrapure laboratory water solutions at Merck KGaA, enterprise operations at Etraveli Group, and network environments—I have cultivated an interdisciplinary expertise where computational workflows and systems engineering intersect.

Outside of data analysis, I am a committed GNU/Linux enthusiast actively developing native desktop applications in Rust (GTK4 / Libadwaita), building automated packaging for Debian/Ubuntu (Arda-Tech PPA), maintaining custom CachyOS Linux kernel trees, and porting Sailfish OS to ARM64 smartphone hardware via the Hardware Adaptation Development Kit (HADK).

Core Pillars

  • Computational Biology & Genomics

  • Linux Kernel & Hardware Adaptation
